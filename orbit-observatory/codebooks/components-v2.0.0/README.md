# Adopted component annotation protocol 2.0.0

Authorized by Badr's adoption prompt. This protocol supersedes the proposed component workflow, not the legacy strict-target codebook. Consolidated-v1 labels, history and W1 remain separate. No labels are migrated, no suggestions approved and no public scores changed.

Each component has its own response. Blank means not reviewed, not no stance. Direction values are support, oppose, mixed, no_stance and unresolved. Mixed requires incompatible commitments on the same target/time/scope; a condition alone is not mixed. No_stance, missing and unresolved are not neutral alignment.

- **Territory:** integrity/sovereignty direction; territorial extent and named regions; separate annexation recognition/non-recognition/mixed/no stance/unresolved and annexation object. Separatist statehood recognition differs from incorporation into Russia.
- **Withdrawal:** withdrawal direction; territorial extent/line; timing and conditions. Partial extent does not invalidate a reviewed withdrawal-direction target. W1 still gates the separate full target.
- **Aid/sanctions:** overall direction and separate measure direction; identify the measure/recipient/target, conditions and restrictions. Define the measure proposition in text so that polarity has a clear referent. Humanitarian aid differs from military assistance. Implementation/effectiveness/nonparticipation does not itself endorse/reject overall sanctions.
- **Responsibility:** direction concerns initiation of February 2022 invasion; preserve causal qualifications and distinguish historical context/incidents/economic effects.

**Explicit** includes clear synonyms and ordinary semantic equivalents. “Equipping Ukraine to defend itself” is explicit military assistance when “equipping” has its ordinary military meaning in the passage. **Contextually implied** requires inference from linked relevant context, with the textual basis explained. Never infer from actor reputation or assumed intentions. Record expression, basis, scope and conditions independently; specificity is not strength.

Source eligibility, attribution, extraction and language quality are separate fields. A missing annexation component cannot disqualify a valid integrity label. Reviewed components become eligible for their own target only with explicit record/component training authorization and source/split gates. Approval of one axis does not approve another. No automatic transfer from strict labels or earlier generic component suggestions.

Examples: Ukraine-integrity affirmation = explicit integrity support, unspecified extent, annexation unaddressed. Russian withdrawal from Ukrainian territory = explicit withdrawal support, extent unspecified, W1 still held. A restricted weapon can have opposition for that measure and support overall aid. A generic peace appeal alone establishes no withdrawal direction.

Aggregation remains separate from public scoring: equal claim/event/quarter hierarchy, separate component coverage/missingness, event-group uncertainty and explicit-only versus implied-inclusive sensitivity. No numerical statement strengths or specificity weights.

For extent, unspecified means the wording does not specify the extent; unresolved means the reviewer cannot settle its interpretation. Neither is weak stance. Leave an unreviewed field blank. A completed integrity judgment does not require completing annexation first; the remaining axis stays independently outstanding.
