# Territorial recognition

Codebook 1.0.0. Apply [the common rules](README.md) first.

Proposition: Ukraine has sovereignty over all its internationally recognized territory; annexation has no legal recognition.

Applicable from 2012-01-01; no fixed end date.

- **support:** Explicitly recognizes Ukrainian sovereignty over its internationally recognized territory/borders, or rejects legal annexation with a clearly specified territorial scope that actually answers the fixed proposition. Generic sovereignty or integrity language alone is insufficient.
- **oppose:** Explicitly recognizes legal Russian annexation or denies Ukrainian legal sovereignty over the territory named in the proposition. Reporting de facto control or a temporary ceasefire line alone is insufficient.
- **no_stance:** Generic invocations of sovereignty, territorial integrity or the UN Charter without resolving the relevant Ukrainian legal territory; peace talks; another country; partial territorial claims that do not settle the full target.
- **mixed:** Endorses and rejects the same Ukrainian legal territorial proposition within an inseparable passage. Split distinct regions/claims first and record scope instead of hiding it in one label.
- **unresolved:** Unclear whether territory refers to internationally recognized Ukrainian borders or a different line; unclear legal recognition versus temporary practical control.

Crimea/referendum material requires exact legal and territorial interpretation. Do not invent an all-territory stance from vague independence language. Qualified or region-specific claims should be preserved for scoped review, not generalized.

## Constructed teaching examples

These examples are invented to explain rules. They are not research evidence, training labels or expected answers to the first batch.

| Illustrative label | Constructed passage |
| --- | --- |
| support | We recognize Ukraine’s sovereignty within its internationally recognized borders. |
| oppose | We recognize the annexed Ukrainian territories as legally part of Russia. |
| no_stance | All states should respect sovereignty. |
| mixed | We accept and reject the very same annexation; this contradictory paragraph needs splitting. |
| unresolved | Those borders should be respected. (Context does not identify which borders.) |
