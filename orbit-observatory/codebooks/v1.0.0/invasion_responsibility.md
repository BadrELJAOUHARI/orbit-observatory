# Responsibility for the February 2022 full-scale invasion

Codebook 1.0.0. Apply [the common rules](README.md) first.

Proposition: Attributes responsibility for the February 2022 full-scale invasion to the Russian state.

Applicable from 2022-02-24; no fixed end date.

- **support:** Explicitly attributes the February 2022 full-scale invasion to the Russian state, its government, military or leadership; identifying Russia as initiating the attack can establish attribution without the word responsibility.
- **oppose:** Explicitly rejects Russian-state responsibility for that invasion. A claim about shared background causes alone does not entail this rejection.
- **no_stance:** Generic conflict language, economic consequences, calls for peace, personal responsibility in another context, or an unendorsed account of another speaker’s claim.
- **mixed:** Inseparable assertion and rejection of Russian responsibility for the same invasion. Criticism of NATO alongside recognition that Russia initiated the invasion need not be mixed.
- **unresolved:** Unclear referent of attack/aggression, date, actor, quoted speaker, irony or whether the assertion concerns 2014 rather than the 2022 full-scale invasion.

Not applicable before 24 February 2022. Rejecting a quoted Kremlin justification can reverse the apparent meaning of a quotation; read the surrounding context and request an expanded span when necessary.

## Constructed teaching examples

These examples are invented to explain rules. They are not research evidence, training labels or expected answers to the first batch.

| Illustrative label | Constructed passage |
| --- | --- |
| support | Russia initiated the full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. |
| oppose | The Russian state bears no responsibility for that invasion. |
| no_stance | The conflict has increased food prices. |
| mixed | Russia initiated this invasion and did not initiate it; both assertions remain unresolved in the passage. |
| unresolved | They started it. (Neither actor nor event is identifiable.) |
