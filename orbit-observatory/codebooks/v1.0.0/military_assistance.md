# Military assistance to Ukraine

Codebook 1.0.0. Apply [the common rules](README.md) first.

Proposition: Supports external military assistance to Ukraine, with type, recipient and conditions preserved.

Applicable from 2012-01-01; no fixed end date.

- **support:** Explicitly endorses or supplies external military equipment, weapons, training or defined military support to Ukraine, retaining type, recipient and conditions. The specified assistance, not the country’s alliance, is the target.
- **oppose:** Explicitly opposes the defined external military assistance to Ukraine; refusal to provide it oneself need not oppose others providing it.
- **no_stance:** Humanitarian/economic aid alone; NATO deterrence deployments outside Ukraine; Ukraine’s abstract self-defence right without a commitment to military assistance; military activity concerning another country.
- **mixed:** Inseparable support and opposition to the same aid definition. Support for defensive arms but opposition to direct combat deployment should normally be split and qualified, not merged into a midpoint.
- **unresolved:** Assistance type, recipient, external supplier, modality or conditions are too unclear to decide whether the defined military policy is endorsed.

Rejecting US combat deployment is not opposition to weapons aid. Do not generalize support for one bounded aid type to every possible weapon or intervention; preserve a narrower target rather than invent a blanket position.

## Constructed teaching examples

These examples are invented to explain rules. They are not research evidence, training labels or expected answers to the first batch.

| Illustrative label | Constructed passage |
| --- | --- |
| support | We support supplying defensive weapons to Ukraine. (Record defensive-weapons scope.) |
| oppose | No state should supply weapons or military training to Ukraine. |
| no_stance | We will provide food and medical relief to Ukraine. |
| mixed | We both support and oppose the same weapons transfer under the same conditions. |
| unresolved | We will provide the necessary assistance. (Type not specified.) |
