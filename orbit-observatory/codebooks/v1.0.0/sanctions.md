# Sanctions against Russia

Codebook 1.0.0. Apply [the common rules](README.md) first.

Proposition: Supports specified restrictive measures against Russia because of its actions in Ukraine.

Applicable from 2012-01-01; no fixed end date.

- **support:** Explicitly supports adopting or maintaining specified restrictive measures against Russia because of its actions in Ukraine. Preserve the measure, target, conditions and temporal state.
- **oppose:** Explicitly opposes adopting/maintaining those Russia-related measures or calls for their removal. Distinguish objections to one measure from all sanctions.
- **no_stance:** Sanctions on Cuba, Zimbabwe, terrorists or another target; descriptions of prices or humanitarian costs alone; a reporter asking about sanctions without an endorsed answer.
- **mixed:** Inseparable support and opposition to the same Russia/Ukraine-linked measure. Normally split different measures, exemptions, adoption and conditional removal.
- **unresolved:** The sanctioned target, Ukraine linkage, measure, stance or conditions are not identifiable from passage and context.

An energy-payment exemption does not negate all sanctions. A threat to impose measures conditional on a future act must retain its condition; do not code it as enacted sanctions.

## Constructed teaching examples

These examples are invented to explain rules. They are not research evidence, training labels or expected answers to the first batch.

| Illustrative label | Constructed passage |
| --- | --- |
| support | We will maintain these restrictions on Russian banks in response to the invasion. |
| oppose | The invasion-related restrictions on Russian banks must be lifted now. |
| no_stance | The embargo on Cuba should end. |
| mixed | We endorse and reject the same banking restriction without a coherent distinction. |
| unresolved | These measures should stop. (Neither target nor measures is clear.) |
