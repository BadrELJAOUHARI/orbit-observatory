# Proposed territorial-lens decomposition

Status: proposal only, 23 October 2026. No approved target, prior label, W1 rule or public score changes.

The current proposition combines three questions: Ukraine's territorial integrity, the extent of territory covered, and the legal recognition of annexation. A statement can support the first without answering the other two. Record these components separately under a future version while retaining the current strict label.

| Component | Proposed responses | Example and consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Integrity direction | support, oppose, no stance, mixed, unresolved | “We uphold Ukraine's territorial integrity” supports direction only. It cannot establish the whole current proposition. |
| Territorial scope | comprehensive internationally recognized territory (1991 borders or explicit equivalent); unspecified Ukrainian territory; named subset; explicit exception; no scope; unresolved | “Within its internationally recognized borders” establishes comprehensive scope. An unqualified reference to Ukraine does not by itself settle Crimea's status. |
| Annexation recognition | explicit non-recognition; recognition; no stance; mixed; unresolved | “We do not recognize Russia's annexation of Crimea” establishes non-recognition for that named annexation; scope across other occupied territories still needs review. |

If approved, retain the existing strict output as a separate field. Strict support requires all three components to establish the full proposition in reviewed, attributable evidence. Explicit annexation recognition can establish strict opposition under the existing negative target. Missing components remain unresolved or no stance under the present codebook. Do not average the components or treat missing annexation language as implicit non-recognition.
